TASK 10 - OBSERVATIONS & INSIGHTS
========================================================================
1. Basic cleaning handles simple formatting issues such as case, punctuation, numbers and extra spaces.
2. Advanced cleaning goes further by removing URLs, email addresses, HTML tags and special characters, producing more consistent text.
3. Stopword removal reduces common words that usually add little information for many NLP tasks.
4. Repeated-character normalization changes informal spellings such as 'soooo' into a shorter standard form, while the slang dictionary maps common chat-style words to clearer forms.
5. Lemmatization is generally preferred when preserving meaningful dictionary words matters because it aims to return a normalized word form rather than only cutting characters.
6. Preprocessing is important because inconsistent raw text creates unnecessary vocabulary variation and noise, which can make later NLP models less effective and harder to interpret.
